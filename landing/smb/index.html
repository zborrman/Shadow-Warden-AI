Your AI Tools Are a Liability

Every employee using ChatGPT, Copilot, or a custom LLM is a potential breach vector. You can't see what's being sent — until it's too late.

🔒

Secrets Leaking

API keys, passwords, and credentials typed into AI prompts are sent to third-party servers you don't control.

⚠️

Prompt Injection

Malicious content in documents, emails, or web pages can hijack your AI agents and exfiltrate company data.

📋

No Audit Trail

When a regulator or customer asks what data was sent to AI systems, you have no answer. That's a compliance failure.
