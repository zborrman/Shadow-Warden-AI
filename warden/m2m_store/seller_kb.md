# Seller assistant knowledge base

Facts the assistant may state, condensed from public Amazon policies (Business
Solutions Agreement, Program Policies) and the EU/US rules they enforce.
Figures that nobody here verified are left out on purpose. Amazon changes its
rules; the assistant always tells the seller to confirm in Seller Central.

## How Amazon enforces
Most actions are automatic reactions to a rule or a metric threshold, not
surprises. Escalation, mildest first:
1. FBA inbound fees and chargebacks: unreadable or missing labels, box
   content that does not match the shipment plan, missing prep.
2. Listing suppression: the item leaves search; usually a missing required
   attribute (main image, product-safety fields for the EU, a title over the
   category's length limit).
3. Losing the featured offer (Buy Box): pricing out of line with other channels
   (Fair Pricing Policy) or rising order defects.
4. ASIN deactivation: restricted claims (pesticide, drug), intellectual-property
   complaints, many quality returns. Reinstatement needs documents or a Plan of
   Action.
5. Inbound suspension: repeated shipment problems; the seller may be limited to
   shipping from their own warehouse.
6. Account deactivation: review manipulation, related accounts, an Account
   Health Rating that falls too low, misuse of buyer data. Reinstatement only by
   appeal; payouts can be held.

## Common seller mistakes
- Listing text: pesticide claims ("antibacterial", "mildew-resistant") need EPA
  registration under FIFRA; disease claims ("cures", "treats eczema") make a
  product a drug (FDA); generic green claims need proof (FTC Green Guides, EU
  Directive 2024/825). Competitors' brand names in titles or search terms risk
  IP complaints. Variations must be the same product in different sizes or
  colours, never different products merged for reviews.
- EU: products sold to EU consumers show the manufacturer, an EU responsible
  person (when the manufacturer is outside the EU) and safety information
  (GPSR). Packaging EPR registration applies in several countries (Germany:
  LUCID).
- FBA prep: since 1 January 2026 Amazon no longer preps or labels units in the
  US. The seller applies the FNSKU label, poly-bag suffocation warnings and
  liquid sealing. Labels must scan: print at 100% scale on a label printer or
  sheet, never shrunk.
- Shipment plans: send exactly the units and box contents the plan says.
- Buyer messages and inserts: no requests for positive reviews, no incentives
  for reviews, no marketing messages, no pushing buyers off Amazon. Amazon's
  buyers are not the seller's marketing list.
- Seller-fulfilled metrics (Amazon's published targets): Order Defect Rate
  under 1%, Late Shipment Rate under 4%, Pre-fulfilment Cancel Rate under 2.5%,
  Valid Tracking Rate above 95%. Cancel only at the buyer's request or for
  reasons Amazon allows; use carriers Amazon recognises for tracking.
- Related accounts: a second seller account needs Amazon's approval; shared
  devices, bank accounts or tax details can link accounts.

## Plan of Action (appeal) structure
Amazon expects three parts: the root cause, the corrective actions already
taken, and the preventive measures that stop it happening again. Specific,
factual, no blame on Amazon or buyers. No result can be promised.

## Tools in this workspace
- Catalog check (Store Builder → Amazon → Catalog check): upload the Active
  Listings Report; barcodes and risky claims checked.
- Listing check: live under a product's description.
- Labels: FNSKU (Code 128) and barcode labels, A4/Letter sheets, rolls, ZPL.
- Account health calculator (Store Builder → Amazon → Account health).
- EU product safety (GPSR) fields on each product, shown on the storefront.
