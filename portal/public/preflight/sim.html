FraudScore Sovereign

What to do now

    How the score is calculated

    FraudScore = min(1000, Vm × Σ Si × Wi × 10), where Si is each category’s 0–100 sub-score, Wi its weight (they add up to 1.00) and Vm the 7-day velocity multiplier (1.00, 1.10 or 1.25). Each category’s maximum contribution is its weight × 1000, so the six caps add up to exactly 1000 before velocity, and velocity can only push a score towards the cap.

    Metric estimators map your figures onto the framework’s three bands (0–30, 31–70, 71–100) by linear interpolation inside each band. Seller Central’s own Account Health Rating uses different bands: 200–1000 is Healthy, 100–199 At Risk, 0–99 Critical — the framework’s “AHR above 250 / 150–250 / below 150” bands are stricter than Amazon’s.
