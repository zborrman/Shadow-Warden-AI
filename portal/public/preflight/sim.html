Listing Preflight

A pre-flight simulator for marketplace sellers: walk through the add-a-product steps, then run the clock 72 hours forward to see which checks would hide the listing, remove the ASIN or put the account at risk.

Independent practice tool — not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon. Nothing leaves your browser. Bot behaviour and thresholds are a simulation built from published policy and seller reports; confirm against current seller help before you publish.
